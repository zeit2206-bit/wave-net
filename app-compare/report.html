note
The learner's version 'I completed the report yesterday' is a mix of past perfect and present perfect tenses. A correct sentence would be 'I finished the report yesterday.' The past perfect tense describes a completed action in the past, while the present perfect tense describes an action that started in the past and continues in the present. The mistake is not present, but rather the incorrect use of the auxiliary verb 'completed' instead of 'finished'. A correct sentence would use 'finished' instead.
